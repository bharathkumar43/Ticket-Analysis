Neutara Ticketing connection

Credentials are stored server-side in config.json (never sent back to the browser in full, never logged). A full sync pulls the entire ticket history and takes several minutes — it runs automatically in the background (every few hours) and can also be triggered on demand below.

Excel upload (Customer Engineering / QA / Infra file-backed tabs)

Expected columns: Issue Type, Key, Summary, Assignee, Reporter, Components, Combination, Priority, Status, Resolution, Created, Updated, Due date, First Response SLA Breach, Resolution SLA Breach. Key prefix decides the team: L2B/L3B → Customer Engineering, IN → Infra, QA → QA. CFITS rows are kept but not attributed to a team (see README — ENT vs SMB can't be split from this sheet alone).
